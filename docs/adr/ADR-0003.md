# ADR-0003: Multilingual song data and content rights

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-08
Decision owner: project owner (explicit product decisions); engineering proposals remain marked.

## Context
Requirements, blueprint and final user clarifications must agree without inventing approval or provider rights.

## Decision
English interface; substantial English/Urdu/Hindi catalogue with Roman text variants, per-component provenance and verified-rights publication gates.

## Alternatives considered
English-only/sample-only library rejected by user. Popularity and Roman script do not establish rights.

## Consequences and follow-up
Full lyrics depend on permission. Use available approved learning data and authorized links; record missing coverage and expand through reviewed imports.
