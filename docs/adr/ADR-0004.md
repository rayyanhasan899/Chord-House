# ADR-0004: Practice calendar policy

Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-11-08
Decision owner: project owner (explicit product decisions); engineering proposals remain marked.

## Context
Requirements, blueprint and final user clarifications must agree without inventing approval or provider rights.

## Decision
Saved sessions drive progress; split active intervals at local midnight; Monday week and current profile timezone recomputation are proposed defaults.

## Alternatives considered
Session-start-day allocation and permanently frozen historical timezone remain alternatives.

## Consequences and follow-up
Test DST, edits/deletion and timezone changes; review before implementing calendar behavior.
