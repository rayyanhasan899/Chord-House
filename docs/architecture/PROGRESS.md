# Progress and learning roadmap

Sign in and open `/progress`, or see the compact summary on `/dashboard`. Only saved sessions contribute. Day/week/month/all-time totals, current/longest streak, current daily/weekly target progress, 28-day chart/table and top linked instruments/songs/chords derive from your recorded activity. A blank history displays zero with a link to Practice, never invented progress.

Calendar follows your current profile timezone. Weeks start Monday; sessions split at local midnight; actual elapsed UTC time handles daylight saving. Any positive saved time qualifies a day, even if displayed minutes round to zero. Exact daily seconds are available to six decimal places. Current streak continues from yesterday until today ends. Editing/deleting practice or changing timezone recalculates results next read. Page navigation/focus/visibility and a 60-second refresh keep summaries current; use Refresh progress immediately after a change in another tab. Review unsaved reflection changes before refreshing. No server-side aggregate cache exists.

Configure/change/pause goals in Practice. Pausing does not remove recorded time. Historical goals are evaluated using the version active at period close; current goals use the present version. No target/paused target has no achievement result. The chart covers 28 calendar days, and current month totals use the full current calendar month. Rankings cover all linked saved sessions, top ten per category; they do not infer time spent on individual links inside a routine or certify learning.

Weekly review selects a completed week by its Monday date, shows recorded time/practice days and evaluated goal results, and lets you save your own improvement note. Weeks are stored as local calendar labels and summary values recalculate in current timezone. Blank and save clears stored text. Stale edits are rejected. An unsaved note pauses background refresh and switching review weeks; save/discard changes first.

Roadmap provides eight original milestones grouped by beginner/intermediate/advanced, prioritizing your selected level with instrument-specific technique guidance. Existing tuner/chords/metronome/pattern/song/practice pages are linked. Mark/unmark milestones yourself; marks are private and stored separately for each selected instrument. Changing skill only reorders suggestions. This is guided self-assessment, not an expert certification or automatic analysis of your playing. No paid AI, audio upload or licensed content is introduced.

API: GET `/api/v1/practice/progress?review_week=YYYY-MM-DD`, GET `/api/v1/practice/roadmap`, PUT `/api/v1/practice/roadmap/{code}` with instrument/completed, PUT `/api/v1/practice/reviews/{week}` with improvement/version. Identity/CSRF/Origin checks and no-store policy reuse Practice. Invalid/current/future/non-Monday review weeks return 422; stale review/instrument changes 409. No owner identifier is accepted from the client. OpenAPI is authoritative.
